Good to know

Pack smart, sleep easy

Bring bedding

Yurts and cabins have real beds but no linens. Pack sheets, pillows and towels.

Shared bathhouse

No private bathroom in yurts or cabins. Clean restrooms and showers are a short walk away.

Light sleeper?

The village sits near the highway and rail line, so pack earplugs for a quiet night.

Pets

Dogs are welcome at RV sites with a dog park on site. Yurts and cabins are pet free.
